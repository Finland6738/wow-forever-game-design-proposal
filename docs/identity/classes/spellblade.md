# Spellblade

[← Class catalog](../classes.md) · [Roles](../class-roles-and-specializations.md) · [Implementation standard](../implementation-standard.md) · [Mechanic coverage](../mechanic-coverage.md)

**Status:** reviewable implementation specification. Three talent trees per class are selected. The named kits below are proposed designs; engine behavior, tuning and final talent allocations still require the evaluation package in the implementation standard.

## Combat identity

Weapon actions prepare stored magic, and the player spends that reserve through a strike, a defensive weave or a rare intervention. The same charge creates an offensive/defensive decision rather than free spell damage added to a full melee kit.

## Roles and equipment

| Talent tree | Primary responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| [Channeler](#channeler) | Melee magical/weapon DPS |
| [Spellguard](#spellguard) | Tank |
| [Oath Duelist](#oath-duelist) | Melee DPS with finite interventions |

**Equipment proposal:** Medium/heavy armor to evaluate; blade and focus, with shield access for the tank tree under review.

The roles describe different build responsibilities, not three full kits active simultaneously. Armor and weapon eligibility are prototype decisions; they do not establish a stronger equipment budget or exclusive race requirement.

## Shared resource and state model

Arcane charges have a cap and are generated by valid weapon/spell sequences. Spellguard adds one primed companion-assisted next action. Oath Duelist adds a finite encounter intervention allowance separate from ordinary charges.

**State progression:** Weave → store → prime an eligible action → consume exactly once → recover. Primed effects expire or are canceled explicitly; they cannot empower every component of one copied attack.

The server accepts one action only after target, range, line of sight, ownership and resource checks. A committed action records its spend once; subsequent hits, visuals and callbacks resolve against that commitment rather than creating new resources or rewards automatically.

## Talent-tree specifications

### Channeler

**Primary role:** Melee magical/weapon DPS. **Design status:** proposed tree specification.

**Decision loop:** Alternate legal weapon and spell actions, store charges and choose a strike sequence or a limited defensive weave.

| Baseline action | Trigger / state change | Guard, trade-off and counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weaving Cut | Use a weapon action to generate a reviewed charge. | No proc recursion that generates more than the valid event permits. |
| Imbued Strike | Consume charges through a deliberate melee release. | One resource spend; additional visual hits share its budget. |
| Defensive Weave | Spend the same reserve on a short self-protective response. | Costs offensive potential and does not supply the tank tree's full survival. |

**Talent anchors to allocate:** Early: steadier generation or a larger stored weave. Middle: sustained sequence versus focused release. Capstone: a finite weapon-channel window with clear break and recovery rules.

These anchors describe node behavior and opportunity costs, not an approved complete point grid. Define ranks, depth, prerequisites and cross-tree interaction before implementing the full tree. The baseline actions constitute the minimum loop to test first.

### Spellguard

**Primary role:** Tank. **Design status:** proposed tree specification.

**Decision loop:** Establish threat, prepare active mitigation and call a small bonded flame/arcane companion to enhance exactly the next eligible attack or guard. Decide which upcoming action deserves the empowerment.

| Baseline action | Trigger / state change | Guard, trade-off and counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guarding Strike | Generate threat and the defensive charge reserve. | Requires ordinary tank positioning and targeting. |
| Prime Companion | Mark one next eligible defensive/offensive action for enhancement. | One primed action, explicit expiry, no independent full pet damage budget. |
| Woven Guard | Consume charges and an eligible priming for bounded mitigation. | An empowered attack instead consumes that same priming; both cannot receive it. |

**Talent anchors to allocate:** Early: mitigation reliability or stronger deliberate commitment. Middle: offense-priming versus protection-priming. Capstone: one reviewed guardian response with a visible cooldown and no encounter immunity.

These anchors describe node behavior and opportunity costs, not an approved complete point grid. Define ranks, depth, prerequisites and cross-tree interaction before implementing the full tree. The baseline actions constitute the minimum loop to test first.

### Oath Duelist

**Primary role:** Melee DPS with finite interventions. **Design status:** proposed tree specification.

**Decision loop:** Build weapon enchantments for ordinary combat, preserve a small encounter intervention allowance and choose when to spend it on damage, a resource response or limited ally assistance.

| Baseline action | Trigger / state change | Guard, trade-off and counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oathcut | Build the ordinary enchantment reserve through melee. | This is separate from the rare encounter allowance. |
| Bound Intervention | Spend one finite allowance on the build's chosen intervention. | No gold generation, reward creation, free unlimited resurrection or failure bypass. |
| Arcane Lunge | Spend ordinary charges for a legal movement/strike action. | Navigable destination, no wall crossing or resetting rare allowances. |

**Talent anchors to allocate:** Early: enchantment consistency versus mobility. Middle: choose an intervention category rather than receive all categories. Capstone: improve one finite intervention at a clear opportunity cost; battle-resurrection access is not assumed.

These anchors describe node behavior and opportunity costs, not an approved complete point grid. Define ranks, depth, prerequisites and cross-tree interaction before implementing the full tree. The baseline actions constitute the minimum loop to test first.

## Failure, interruption and recovery

Range/target failure cannot consume and refund the same charge twice. A primed companion intervention is a bounded class effect, not another geared pet. Encounter reset, not leaving personal combat, governs the rare allowance.

Target death, owner death, encounter reset, dispel and reconnect must use published recovery rules. An action that delivered its full benefit cannot also return its entire unused cost. Distinguish a canceled commitment from a spent one, and expose the result in the interface.

## Group and activity integration

Channeler and Oath Duelist are melee damage; Spellguard is a tank proposal. The companion-empowers-next-action and finite-intervention studies have explicit homes here rather than being lost between Summoner variants.

Apply permanent equipment, milestone Rune unlocks and the one-slot Adventurer Repertoire through the same effective-power review. Normal/Veteran contextual bonuses stay in scope. Owned effects inherit the approved normalized profile, and valid control/protection/objective contribution receives owner-level credit without multiplying player shares.

## Implementation parameters and interface

Before an engine prototype, fill the parameter sheet with resource cap/start state, generation events and rate, action costs, cooldowns, target caps, range/line-of-sight rules, durations, coefficients, control categories and refund/return fractions. The behaviors above constrain those values; none permits an unlimited default.

Display the shared reserve, committed state, selected target and the tree's next important window. Inputs must work at ordinary network latency and without an addon explaining hidden enemy state. Talent respec or equipment swaps cannot preserve mutually exclusive active benefits or reset encounter allowances.

## Acceptance scenes

1. Prime the companion, cast a multi-component action and consume the enhancement exactly once.
2. Drop personal combat during an active boss and preserve the spent intervention allowance.
3. Compare Spellguard survival and Channeler output without a hybrid retaining both full kits.
4. Exercise death, reconnect, target loss and encounter reset with no duplicate spend, proc, reward or free cooldown reset.
5. Compare baseline and normalized profiles, including representative Rune/repertoire interactions and any contextual raid bonuses.
6. Allocate mixed-tree builds at the approved level-60 talent budget; verify they do not combine full role outputs or bypass a capstone's intended opportunity cost.

## Implementation handoff and open decisions

The first deliverable is the shared resource/state loop plus the three baseline actions for one tree. Demonstrate it in a single-target scene, a multi-target pull, movement, failure recovery and counterplay, then expand the other trees against the same contribution contract.

The final point grid, coefficients, equipment restrictions, animation/effect costs, race/class matrix and competitive policies remain open. Review measured output, survival, resource pressure, input load, readability and group access before promoting this specification to a validated implementation candidate. The [standard](../implementation-standard.md) defines the evidence gate.

## Shared progression boundary

These are ordinary class talent-tree abilities, usable according to their own combat rules. The separate final-boss Adventurer Repertoire reward slot works only in matching-difficulty raids (Normal to Normal, Veteran to Veteran), never in dungeons, open world or PvP. Rune milestone records grant no cumulative combat statistics. Apply the [shared implementation standard](../implementation-standard.md) to this kit and its hybrids.
