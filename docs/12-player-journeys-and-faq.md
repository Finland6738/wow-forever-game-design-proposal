# Player journeys and frequently asked questions

[← Main proposal](../README.md) · [Documentation index](README.md) · [Decision register](13-glossary-and-decisions.md)

## A new level-60 character

The character completes a finite preparation path through accessible quests, dungeons or professions and obtains an adequate permanent equipment profile. They can then join the current raid without clearing every earlier update. This does not guarantee a clear: encounter learning, group roles and coordination still matter.

Alternative preparation sources are part of the contract. No particular World Boss, territorial victory, prestige tier or old raid item may be the only realistic route to entry-ready power.

## A returning raider after several updates

A player returns during Update C with an adequate permanent set from Update A. They retain their items, appearance collection and Update A local progress. They can start Update C directly; Update A contextual bonuses remain in Update A and do not activate in C.

If their build needs adjustment, accessible sidegrades can provide it. The preparation journey should not silently become “replace every slot with this update's stronger baseline.” Measure real acquisition sources and time, rather than only whether the item-level number stayed constant.

## A Veteran-only guild

The guild chooses Veteran and begins from the common permanent baseline. Normal bonuses do not apply. The guild earns its own Veteran contextual rewards and attempts the stated encounters until it succeeds or decides to change its approach.

No Normal currency or exclusive recipe accelerates its Veteran power track. Playing Normal with friends remains allowed; it simply does not become a required second gearing schedule.

## A healer learning a Challenge dungeon

The group uses accessible normalized profiles, chooses a route and begins a ranked attempt. A wipe does not consume a key. They reset and practice again. The healer receives eligible participation recognition through meaningful healing, control or objectives rather than damage placement.

A disconnect keeps the timer running. The same character may reconnect under the recovery policy. Replacing a participant invalidates that ranked attempt, and the group may start a fresh run with the new roster.

## A settlement is lost while someone is questing

The original village becomes visibly damaged and local NPCs withdraw. Essential services and quest continuity move to the defined fallback camp. The player can help gather supplies, repair structures or recruit replacement service roles.

Their existing quest progress remains valid. If population is low, NPC assistance and the configured recovery deadline restore essential functions. Nobody must sacrifice a week of play because they missed a defense event.

## A procedural expedition discovers a deeper floor

The expedition seed committed one to five floors at creation. After completing the current objectives, the group discovers an entrance if another floor exists. It chooses whether to continue, keeping legitimately earned checkpoint rewards if it leaves.

The next floor validates its 5–20-player roster and commits a scaling profile. A death or departure during a boss does not downscale the encounter. Reconnects preserve the same run and seed. Late replacements cannot claim floors they did not participate in.

## A player prefers exploration and civic activity

They progress through stories, landmarks, investigations, settlement defense and reconstruction, earning Explorer recognition. Repeatedly greeting an NPC or crossing a boundary produces no new objective credit.

Their identity is worthwhile without being a raid-power track. They can join group world activities or later prepare for a raid through the same accessible equipment routes. Their Explorer tier does not provide stronger statistics or become an activity-entry gate.

## Does horizontal progression mean no upgrades?

No. The initial permanent preparation journey contains upgrades, and raids have capped local progression. The difference is where that power applies. Later releases do not automatically increase the permanent budget, and one update's local bonuses do not become another update's entry requirement.

## Does everyone receive every reward?

No. Activity access, eligible participation and completing a demanding objective are separate. Veteran prestige and Challenge medals require the stated success. Accessible preparation should support an attempt, not replace the accomplishment.

## Why keep Normal and Veteran separate?

The intention is a choice of commitment. If Normal provides required Veteran power, groups feel pressure to play both. Separate contextual tracks preserve a direct Veteran path while leaving both activities available socially.

## Will an old raid remain useful forever?

Its story, collections, achievements and local progression remain available. That creates reasons to revisit it, but does not guarantee enough groups at every hour. Group formation and participation still need evaluation. Exclusive old combat advantages would undermine the direct-entry promise.

## Are participation ranks a skill rating?

No. They record eligible accumulated activity. A high total can reflect time played and activity choice. Competitive records and specific achievements communicate other accomplishments and should remain distinct.

## Can several ranks or bonuses combine into more permanent power?

No. Prestige has no combat or yield bonus. Raid effects are validated by scope and difficulty, then constrained by effect-family and context budgets. The effective-power policy also covers procs, pets, consumables and temporary effects.

## Are all concept names and numerical values final?

No. Invented working names identify proposed peoples, classes and mechanical studies. The five-to-twenty-player expedition envelope and one-to-five-floor depth are requested parameters; scoring weights and suggested time windows are prototypes. Loot rates, caps, thresholds and exact tuning require evidence.

## Where should a correction be made?

Edit the relevant Markdown in the main repository and submit the change through the normal review process. A merge into `main` updates the wiki automatically. The wiki is the reading view; [wiki maintenance](14-wiki-maintenance.md) describes synchronization, validation and recovery.
