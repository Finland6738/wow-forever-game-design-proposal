# Reward boundaries, acquisition and ownership

[← Main proposal](../README.md) · [Documentation index](README.md) · [Technical framework](04-technical-framework.md)

## Separate access, reward eligibility and prestige

Being allowed to enter an activity, qualifying for a reward and earning a difficult achievement are different decisions. Unlimited Challenge attempts do not imply duplicate rewards for a replayed completion message. An accessible raid baseline does not imply that a group automatically completes Veteran.

This page collects cross-system policies. Exact drop rates, prices, cooldowns and weekly allowances remain open where they are not specified elsewhere.

## Reward matrix

| Activity | Permanent equipment | Contextual power | Economic rewards | Recognition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quests and baseline preparation | Approved entry-ready options | None required for raids | Bounded ordinary rewards | Story and exploration milestones |
| Heroic dungeon | Budgeted baseline alternatives and sidegrades | No raid-track unlock required | Materials and reviewed recipes | Collections and Adventurer participation |
| Raid Normal | Permanent profile inside the common budget | Its update's Normal track only | Optional reviewed materials/recipes | Normal completion and collections |
| Raid Veteran | Same permanent budget | Its update's Veteran track only | No required advantage for another path | Veteran achievements and prestige |
| Challenge Mode | No power reward track | None | No raid-equivalent weekly reward | Medals, records and cosmetic collections |
| Procedural expedition | No proposed equipment ladder | None | Modest gold/common materials to test | Discovery cosmetics and Adventurer participation |
| Territorial World Boss | Budgeted optional crafting alternatives | No exclusive raid prerequisite | Reviewed artifacts/materials with reasonable alternatives | Event and collection rewards |
| Settlement defense/recovery | No exclusive baseline requirement | None | Event supplies fund restoration | Explorer civic commendations |
| Territorial PvP | No compulsory PvE equipment route | Regional activity effects only where permitted | Local rewards with PvE alternatives for needed resources | Honor/PvP recognition |
| Prestige ranks | None from rank itself | None | No yield or throughput bonus from rank | Titles, badges and cosmetics |

An approved recipe is not automatically harmless: its output and acquisition effort need review. An optional material becomes a practical obligation if it is the only realistic source of a superior combat effect.

## Equivalent routes must be reasonable

Functional alternatives should be available in achievable time and at a reasonable cost for the intended player. A rare auction-house listing is insufficient evidence. Compare preparation paths by role, activity preference, population and access schedule.

Equivalence need not erase visual identity. A settlement reward can have a unique appearance while an accessible crafted item offers comparable combat function. Specialization remains valuable when it creates choices within the budget rather than access to a stronger budget.

## Raid acquisition and lockouts

Normal and Veteran have distinct contextual tracks. A currency, recipe, upgrade token or account unlock must not transfer power between them. Shared cosmetics may remain account-wide if that does not affect combat or access.

Within an update, sibling raids share a contextual budget. Each needs a self-contained acquisition route; exclusive required unlocks and additive per-raid caps would recreate a prerequisite ladder. Their exact loot cadence is an open pilot decision, not a finalized universal weekly cap.

Compare at least two arrangements: independently earnable equivalent rewards that fill one capped track, and a shared capped progression allowance with a choice of raid sources. Evaluate double-farming pressure, shorter-raid efficiency, accidental allowance consumption and the experience of groups that enjoy multiple raids.

Local catch-up may shorten preparation for a late joiner to an existing update. It cannot require an earlier update or a lower difficulty. It should also preserve worthwhile progression for the group already playing that content.

## Gold and material generation

Gold rewards add currency even when no stronger item drops. Measure expected gold per player-hour, total gold created, group-size distribution, bot exposure and the value of repeatable short routes. Do not use exponential floor rewards to make deeper expeditions attractive.

Materials affect supply, profession demand and access. Monitor output by resource and activity, not only gold-equivalent value. Avoid unique progression-critical materials from random deep floors or faction-dominated events. Bounded reward generation and reasonable alternatives are distinct requirements.

Event reconstruction supplies can be local-purpose resources rather than rare trade materials. Deliveries should consume only the accepted amount. A finished project must not eat inventory or issue rewards again after a retry.

## Ownership and duplicate prevention

| Entitlement | Starting ownership policy | Duplicate boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent item | Existing reviewed item/trade policy | No collection archive duplication of tradable items |
| Contextual progression | Intended character, update and difficulty | No alt funnel or cross-difficulty conversion |
| Appearance/cosmetic | Account-wide where supported | One collection entitlement, with separate acquisition history if needed |
| Adventurer/Explorer points | Per-character cumulative total | One valid source occurrence, plus explicit repeat rules |
| Settlement recognition | Per participant within one canonical event allowance | Defense and recovery do not create two unrestricted payouts |
| Expedition floor reward | Character participation in that expedition/floor | No repeated claims, inherited earlier floors or seed rerolls |
| Challenge record | Validated run identity and ruleset | Reconnects and duplicate completion requests cannot generate a new record |

Persist authoritative entitlements before confirming completion. If recovery requires a retry, return the original result rather than reissuing the reward. Technical failures should not create a choice between lost legitimate progress and duplicate grants.

## Preserve earned meaning

Cosmetic does not mean automatic. A title, medal or appearance can represent a demanding objective without granting permanent combat power. Collections also need clear criteria and understandable progress; extreme rarity alone does not establish meaningful prestige.

When correcting an unintended powerful effect, preserve ownership and collections where possible and communicate the functional change. A permanent budget can require balance corrections without making each content update a planned gear replacement cycle.
