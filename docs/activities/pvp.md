## Shared PvP boundaries

Territorial objectives should create meaningful group conflict without becoming a preparation tax for raids or Challenge Dungeons. Regional combat benefits do not transfer into those activities. Necessary PvE materials must have reasonable alternatives to faction-controlled sources.

Participation follows the relevant ruleset. Where consent is required, the event must communicate entry and exit clearly. High-level objectives should avoid essential leveling routes and should not reward farming low-level or non-participating characters.

PvP Honor remains separate from PvE kills and settlement defense. Hostile [NPC invasions](../06-invasions-and-reconstruction.md) are cooperative PvE events; they do not themselves activate a new PvP participation state.
