# Arenas and Gladiator Standing

[← PvP](pvp.md) · [Recognition](../08-prestige-ranks.md) · [Challenge Dungeons](challenge-dungeons.md)

## Identity and scope

Gladiator identifies the arena-competition domain in the proposed recognition structure. It is distinct from cumulative PvP Honor, Adventurer participation and the time-based Challenger standings of Challenge Dungeons.

This page records that requested separation. Team sizes, rating calculation, matchmaking, seasons, title thresholds and the exact arena format remain open; no existing game's implementation is assumed.

## Competitive boundaries

Arena recognition should reflect the defined competitive result rather than raw PvE kills or Rune completion. Numerical [Rune progression](../systems/runes.md), the [Adventurer Repertoire](../systems/adventurer-abilities.md), equipment and class/racial utility need an explicit competitive policy before ranked play is specified.

Different policies trade earned-character identity against equal access to competitive options. Do not silently assume that all collectible abilities are enabled, all are granted, or none are usable. That choice remains to be discussed alongside the repertoire rules.

Arena participation does not select a city allegiance or activate the separate [Faction Warfare](faction-warfare.md) flag. Service reputation for a city is not an arena rating.
