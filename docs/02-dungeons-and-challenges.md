# Dungeons — Choose an activity

[← Main proposal](../README.md) · [Topic directory](README.md)

Three dungeon formats serve different goals. Open the page for the activity you are looking for.

| Activity | Group | Pace and difficulty | Equipment and rewards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Heroic Dungeons (HC)](activities/heroic-dungeons.md) | Five level-60 players | Untimed tactical pulls, fixed redesigned encounters | Permanent baseline alternatives, materials, recipes and collections |
| [Challenge Dungeons](activities/challenge-dungeons.md) | Five level-60 players; fixed ranked roster | Timed, normalized, fixed encounter difficulty with free retries | Medals, records and prestige; no raid-power track |
| [Procedural Dungeons](07-procedural-expeditions.md) | 5–20 level-60 players | Untimed generated routes, packs and bosses over 1–5 finite floors | Gold/material options to test, discoveries and participation prestige |

## Shared boundaries

All three support cooperation without an endless permanent gear ladder. Heroic and procedural play do not become required reward tracks for Challenge competition. Entry preparation, reward eligibility and earning a difficult achievement remain separate decisions.

For implementation constraints, see the [technical framework](04-technical-framework.md). For economy and ownership, see [rewards and acquisition](11-rewards-and-acquisition.md).
