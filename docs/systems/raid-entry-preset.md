# Raid Entry Preset

[← Raids](../01-progression-and-raids.md) · [Equipment](equipment-and-progression.md) · [Raid talents](adventurer-abilities.md)

## A finite transition after level 60

A newly level-60 character completes an accessible preparation step to earn a **role-appropriate raid-entry equipment preset**. It becomes the lasting default starting set for each new raid season/update. Its permanent budget does not rise with each release.

This is a finite transition into endgame, not an automatic claim that reaching 60 grants a complete set instantly. Exact acquisition tasks, time, item representation and costs remain open. The route must be understandable and independent of historical raid clears, a lower raid difficulty, Warfare victory or accumulated Rune statistics.

## Ownership and new seasons

| Situation | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Newly level 60 | Has access to the preparation route |
| Preset earned | Keeps the entitlement/equipment and role profile |
| A new raid update opens | Uses the same permanent entry budget; no new entry-set grind |
| Returning after several updates | Can use the retained entry set or finish the same accessible route |
| Normal or Veteran entry | Same equipment entry budget; difficulty comes from encounter execution |
| Role/build change | Needs an accessible compatible profile, without multiplying the full preparation journey unnecessarily |

Publish weapon behavior, armor, stats, procs, enchants, consumables and relevant profession effects in the entry profile. Equivalent reviewed items may remain useful; the preset must not make an unseen permanent-stat tier necessary. Normal/Veteran contextual equipment still follows its own bounded scope and cannot increase the next update's entry requirement.

## Presentation and controls

Show which equipment/profile is selected and why it satisfies raid entry preparation. Do not silently replace the player's appearance or destroy earned items. Whether the system stores a physical equipment set, an entitlement with loadout retrieval or another representation is an implementation choice.

The raid preset is not automatically an Arena or Challenge normalization profile. Those modes have their own explicit equipment policies. Loot and older raid collections retain identity and useful choices without forcing a sequential item-level climb.

## Acceptance cases

1. A prepared newcomer enters the current raid without farming earlier raid tiers or usage-based statistics.
2. A returning character uses the same entry budget after several updates; no higher default set is issued.
3. A Veteran group starts from the entry set without first clearing Normal for equipment access.
4. A player switches role through an accessible compatible profile; exact entitlement and acquisition rules are visible.
5. Acquiring contextual raid bonuses or historical talents does not silently alter the permanent preset budget.
