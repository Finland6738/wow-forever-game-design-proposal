# Change history

## 0.4 — 2026-11-10

- Reorganized the wiki around activities and reader interests, with categorized Home, sidebar and detailed topic index.
- Split dedicated pages for World Bosses, Heroic Dungeons, Challenge Dungeons, Open-World PvP, Races and Classes; retained concise overview pages and corrected incoming section links.
- Added clear entry pages for PvP, Faction Warfare, professions, exploration and equipment/progression.
- Added a configurable navigation map with stable page names, automatic discovery of unlisted pages and validation of moved or missing topics.
- Recorded a separate review of the incoming endgame, Rune, Adventurer-ability and province-campaign reports. Those new mechanics remain unadopted pending design decisions.

## 0.3 — 2026-11-10

- Added the author's background and an explicit commitment to group play, simple rules, meaningful challenge and earned prestige.
- Removed the cover caption and design comparisons with other games; introduced invented working names and self-contained concept descriptions.
- Expanded the documentation with social principles, reward/acquisition policies, player journeys, FAQ, glossary, decision register and wiki-maintenance guidance.
- Added an automatically generated GitHub Wiki with navigation, section links and publishing checks; the repository Markdown remains the source for concept updates.

## 0.2 — 2026-11-10

- Added NPC-faction invasions, cooperative settlement defense, reconstruction and NPC restoration, with service continuity and anti-sabotage reward rules.
- Added world-portal procedural expeditions for 5–20 level-60 players, generated routes/packs/bosses and 1–5 finite floors, with reward options and scaling safeguards.
- Added separate Adventurer, Explorer and Honor prestige, prototype level-based kill values, role-aware eligibility and no statistical rewards.
- Added the future-region and playable-identity policy, six exploratory peoples, 18 class concept families and 13 mechanical studies; consolidated overlapping ideas and flagged identity conflicts for redesign.
- Updated the README, technical contract, evaluation roadmap, source register and unpublished forum summary; added a settlement lifecycle diagram.

## 0.1 — 2026-11-10

- Established the public repository as the detailed home of the community proposal.
- Consolidated permanent equipment with update-wide, difficulty-scoped raid bonuses and independent Normal/Veteran progression.
- Documented tactical Heroics, keyless Challenge Modes, elite regions, territorial PvP, professions and other optional activities.
- Added conceptual technical rules, acceptance cases, trade-offs, a pilot roadmap and an official-source register.
- Added original cover art, editable explanatory diagrams, contribution guidance and an unpublished forum summary draft.

This version describes a design proposal. It does not ship gameplay systems or represent an official roadmap.
